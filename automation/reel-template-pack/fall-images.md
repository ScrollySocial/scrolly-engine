## Rules for every image

- Photorealistic, soft even light, at least 2000 px, no text, brand names or logos anywhere.
- Objects: on a **pure white background**, whole object in frame (not cropped), no cast shadow, so they cut out clean.
- Textures (blanket, sweater): fill the whole frame edge to edge, shot straight down, no background showing.
- Make all the versions of one thing **in the same ChatGPT chat** ("the same pie, now with one slice missing"), so they
  match and can animate from one to the next.
- Many of a thing, each a bit different (framework rule 9): ask for 3 to 4 separate versions, not one sheet of many.

Base prompt for objects: *"Photorealistic studio product photo of [object], [angle], isolated on a pure white
background, soft even lighting, no shadow, whole object in frame, no text or logos."*
