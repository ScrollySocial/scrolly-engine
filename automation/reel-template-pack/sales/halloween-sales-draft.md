## 3. How it works

1. **Film your clips.** Hold your phone upright and start each clip right on the action. The recipe card says what to film and for how long.
2. **Drop them in.** Unzip the template and put your clips in the folder *1 Put your clips here*, named 1, 2, 3 in the order on the card.
3. **Double-click the installer.** *2 Double-click to install* copies the template into CapCut under a new name and adds your clips. It never touches your other projects.
4. **Open CapCut.** The template is on the home screen. Double-click each text box, type over the [brackets], and export at 1080p.
5. **Add a sound in Instagram** and post.

## 4. Who it's for

Online business owners and creators who want a Halloween Reel that sells something: a product, a sale, a freebie
or an offer. It works for physical products, digital products and services, since every clip is your own footage.

The full template installs in CapCut for computer on a Windows PC, and a phone kit is included for CapCut on your phone.

## 5. FAQ

**Do I need CapCut on a computer?**
Yes. The templates open in CapCut for computer (the free download). Install it and open it once before you run the installer.

**Does it work on a Mac?**
Not yet. The installer is Windows only for now. [MAC: add a waitlist link or date if you decide on one.]

**Can I use it on my phone?**
Yes. Every template comes with a phone kit: the shot guide video, the recipe card and the
graphics as PNG images. In the CapCut phone app you add your clips and words, then place the graphics as overlays.
The full animation is built for you only in the computer version.

**Is music included?**
No sound files are included, so your Reel never gets muted for a song you don't have the rights to. Each template
suggests a sound [SOUND PICKS: list once chosen]. Add it in Instagram after you upload: on a business account,
Instagram only shows sounds cleared for business use.

**Do I need to install any fonts?**
No. The big Halloween lettering is part of the artwork. The words you edit use Arial, which comes with Windows, or
Bodoni Moda, which comes inside the template.

**How do I change the words?**
Double-click a text box in CapCut and type over the [brackets]. Keep it to a few words so people can read it in time.

**Windows says it protected my PC. Is that normal?**
Yes, Windows shows this for installers it hasn't seen before. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

**What if I want a different clip in one shot?**
Right-click the clip on CapCut's timeline, choose Replace, and pick another.
