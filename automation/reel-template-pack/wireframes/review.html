04. A or B?

World: the weighing table: a real brass balance scale on terracotta terrazzo, a blush and a sage paint card as the two tones
Job: Sellers with two versions: get people to pick one in the comments (engagement + a buying nudge).
Moves: Two-tone strip slice · Balance scale tips to the side on screen
Lettering: A and B in Alfa Slab filled with painted dots (blush / sage); PICK ONE in black-dot letters
CTA: Comment [A] or [B]; Both at [shop]

1. 0-2 s · Hook

Background: terrazzo (images/Warm terracotta terrazzo texture-3.png) shows in the gaps

Footage goes: clip 1 (both versions) cut into 8 tall strips, every other strip tinted blush / sage

Main action: the strips slide up and down into line until they make one clip

Doodles: a marker burst behind OR

Carried on: the strips

Transition out: the strips slide apart again and clip A fills the screen

Text: A, OR, B built-in; [Which one are you?] editable

2. 2-5 s · Version A, full frame

Background: none: the clip fills the frame

Footage goes: full screen

Main action: the brass scale bottom right tips down towards A

Doodles: arrow from the card to the product; wavy underline under the name; a sparkle

Carried on: the scale, bottom right

Transition out: strip slice: clip A cuts into flat strips and B slides in between

Text: A: [name] [one word] editable, on the blush paint card

Background: blush and sage tones on alternate strips

Footage goes: clip A strips and clip B strips, alternating

Main action: A strips slide left out, B strips slide in from the right

Doodles: none (one action)

Carried on: the scale (now level)

Transition out: B strips join into one clip

Text: none

Carried on: the scale

Transition out: hard cut on the beat to the words card
