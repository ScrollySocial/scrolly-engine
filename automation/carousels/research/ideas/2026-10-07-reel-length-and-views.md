## The insight
In Socialinsider's study of 6 million brand Reels (January to June 2026), Reels of 45 to 60 seconds got the most median views (10,374) and Reels under 30 seconds got the fewest (4,700), with a sharp drop after 3 minutes; short Reels still have their own strengths.

## Why it's worth a carousel
- Who it matters to: anyone planning Reels to reach their views number.
- What it helps them do: choose a length on purpose (explain/demonstrate longer; post short ones often).
- What's interesting: it challenges "shorter is always better".
- 5+ slides: the common belief → the data by length bracket → the sweet spot → the 3-minute drop → when short still wins (her Playbook: micro-Reels are quick, so you can post more and test formats) → how to pick.
- Visual angle: a bar chart of median views by length.
- Consistent with her method: yes, Playbook ch. 9 states the same finding and the short-Reel strengths. Keep that balance (her weekly Reels engine favors short clips).
