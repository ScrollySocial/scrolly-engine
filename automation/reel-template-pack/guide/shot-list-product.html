Shot list: Product Reel Template Pack

Every clip for all 25 templates on one list. Film the shots that repeat once, then tick off each template as you go.

Filming

  • Hold your phone upright (vertical), near a window, leaned on something.
  • Start each clip right on the action. Film a few seconds longer than listed.
  • Keep your product in the middle. Words go at the top and bottom.
  • "You on camera" clips are easiest with a helper holding the phone.

Then, for each template

  1. Name its clips 1, 2, 3, in the order listed.
  2. Put them in its folder 1 Put your clips here.
  3. Double-click 2 Double-click to install, then type over the [brackets] in CapCut.
