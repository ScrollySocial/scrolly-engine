Accessibility Statement

Scrolly SEO is committed to making its website and digital services usable by as many people as possible.

Our commitment

We are working toward alignment with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA and continue to improve the usability of our pages, forms, documents, and purchasing experience.

Need assistance?

If you experience difficulty using this website, use the form below. Please include the page URL, a description of the problem, and the browser or assistive technology you were using when possible.

Ongoing improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We review reported barriers and use that feedback to improve the experience. This statement does not claim that every part of the site is fully conformant in every technology or circumstance.
