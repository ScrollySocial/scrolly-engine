# Reel Template Pack framework (read before every template, check before every showing)

Mercedes's rules for every Reel template in every pack (physical, digital, service, Halloween, fall, Thanksgiving,
Christmas). She has had to repeat these many times. **Every session working on a template reads this file first,
designs to it, and runs the checklist at the bottom before showing her anything.** If a storyboard fails one check,
fix it first; don't show it. When she adds a rule, add it here the same day.

## The rules (Mercedes, 2026-10-17, her words tidied)

1. **Visual hook instantly.** Frame 1 is already interesting and moving: no empty opener, no title card that sits there.
2. **Video-focused.** We are making Reels, not carousels. Footage (the buyer's clips) is on screen most of the time;
   graphics serve the video and move with it.
3. **Bold, creative fonts.** Lettering filled with patterns, magazine-style type, letters made of fun shapes or with
   fun backgrounds behind them (used sparingly). Never a plain default font as the hero.
4. **Emphasis on the transitions between clips.** Every clip change is a designed moment, not a hard cut by default.
5. **Interesting ways to split the screen.** Diagonals, shapes, windows, torn edges, objects that divide the frame.
6. **Layered backgrounds that change and help transition between videos.** The background is never the same all Reel;
   its layers move, peel, slide or open to carry one clip into the next.
7. **Oversized elements.** Big props, big words, things larger than life and partly off the frame.
8. **Outlined visuals.** Real images with what looks like a hand-drawn line around them.
9. **Real objects, played with, and many of them.** Not just an apple: an apple with an outline, a polka-dot overlay,
   a sticker or embossed label on it. Then lots of apples, each a little different.
10. **A blend of real photos, drawings and animation.** Real photographed objects (never objects drawn in code), a
    drawn playful layer (outlines, doodles, tape), and motion on everything.
11. **Text that looks handwritten** in places, as if someone is writing it as you watch (still readable over video).
12. **Real-looking paper:** post-it notes, note cards, letters, lined paper, labels.
13. **Every Reel has a purpose for someone who runs a business on Instagram** (an influencer or a business owner):
    a clear business job (sell the product, show quality, answer a buying question). Each pack has one audience:
    **the current pack is for physical product owners only.**

## Learned on templates 01 to 04 and the Halloween pack (keep)

- **Each template has its own world:** don't reuse another template's colours, backdrop or playful elements.
  #2 = kraft box, sage, washi tape. #3 = corkboard, push pins, label-maker tape, mint camera.
- **No 100% solid-colour backgrounds**, and never plain white. Use real textured backdrops, layered.
- **Layers interact with the video:** objects pass over it, the video breaks out of a frame, a prop half off-screen
  and see-through over the clip, the clip develops as a timer counts.
- **The story is logical frame to frame.** Act out the template's idea step by step (packing: label, folding, products
  in, extras, sealing, mailing). Don't show a result before its cause; don't repeat a frame.
- **Readability over video:** handwriting fonts (Ink Free) can't be read over footage on their own. Use bold type with
  a dark outline and a soft dark band, or put the words on real paper (note, tape, label).
- **No blurry hero props.** No clipart next to real photos. Nothing flying at the lens like it's being spat out.
- **Rejected looks:** code-drawn objects (boxes, magnifiers), emoji as main graphics, coloured outlines on objects in
  #2 (white die-cut edges only there), pink washi, Imbue condensed in cobalt with a glow.
- **Fonts and colours come from the scene's props** (e.g. the mint of the camera), and the accent text colour is the
  one buyers change to their brand.
- **CTA:** "Comment [KEYWORD]" (ManyChat style) is the default for most templates.
- **Sound matters:** recommend sounds per beat that are safe for business accounts; don't bundle files we can't license.
- **Process (she is short on time):** one concept, then the exact list of real images she makes in ChatGPT, then ONE
  storyboard round of stills built from those images with all feedback applied in one pass, then animate. Ask
  instead of guessing.

## Checklist: run before showing any storyboard or preview

- [ ] Frame 1 hooks instantly and is already moving (rule 1).
- [ ] Footage is on screen most of the time; this is a Reel, not a carousel (2).
- [ ] The hero type is bold and creative (pattern, magazine, shapes), and readable (3).
- [ ] Every clip change is a designed transition (4).
- [ ] At least one interesting split screen (5).
- [ ] The background changes and its layers carry the transitions (6).
- [ ] Something is oversized (7).
- [ ] Real objects have hand-drawn outlines or play on them, and there are many of them, each a bit different (8, 9).
- [ ] Real photos + drawings + animation are all present; no object is drawn in code (10).
- [ ] Some words look handwritten; some sit on real-looking paper (11, 12).
- [ ] Its world is its own (not another template's look); the story is logical frame to frame.
- [ ] It has a clear business purpose for this pack's audience (physical product owners) (13).
- [ ] Would someone say "wow, I've never seen that on my feed"? If not, it isn't ready.
