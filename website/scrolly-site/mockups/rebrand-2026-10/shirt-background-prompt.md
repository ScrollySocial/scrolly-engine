## Prompt (portrait 4:5)

A handmade torn-paper collage of a dreamy daytime sky, layered like stacked strips of torn art paper, each strip with a
soft cream deckled paper edge (#f7f3ec) and visible paper grain, slight crinkle texture, like a vintage magazine collage.
Palette: sky blue (#4da3ff), pale blue mist (#dfe4f0), a few deeper navy (#0a1930) strips at the very bottom, cream
paper edges. Soft torn-paper clouds drift across the top and the sides, with gentle swirling wind lines cut from paper,
a quiet nod to Van Gogh brushwork. A few small foil-stamped sparkle stars in gold and silver, four-point and
eight-point, only in the top corners. The centre of the image is calm and simple, a smooth pale blue area with soft
texture, leaving clear empty space for a product to be placed later. No people, no animals, no clothing, no text, no
lettering, no logos, no sun. Surreal, high fashion, editorial, airy, handcrafted, flat lighting, shot straight on like a
scanned collage. Portrait format.

**Size:** 4:5 (for example 1600 × 2000). Midjourney: `--ar 4:5 --style raw --v 7`
