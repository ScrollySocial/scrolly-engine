Put your clips in this folder, then double-click "2 Double-click to install".

They go into the template in name order, so name them 1 to 3 (for example 1.mov, 2.mov).
Film upright (vertical) and start each clip right on the action.

1: The before (at least 3 seconds)
2: The after (at least 3 seconds)
3: The product in your hand (at least 3 seconds)
