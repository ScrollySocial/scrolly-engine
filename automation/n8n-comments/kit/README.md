# Chat Manager

Your own comment and DM automation on your own small server: people comment or message a keyword and get your
freebie, sign up with their email, and every comment from Instagram, YouTube and TikTok lands in one inbox for you to
answer.

## Start here

1. Open this folder in Claude Code (the desktop app's Code tab, or `claude` in a terminal here).
2. Say: **set up my Chat Manager**.

Claude asks a few questions, then either clicks through the Meta, Google and DigitalOcean pages with you (Claude in
Chrome) or opens the step-by-step guide (`guide/index.html`). You sign in, paste keys into a page on your own
computer and press the approve buttons; Claude does the rest. Plan on about an hour.

## What you need

- Claude Code on a paid Claude plan.
- A DigitalOcean account with a card: the server is $6 a month.
- An Instagram Professional account (Creator or Business) and a Facebook login for Meta's developer site.
- Optional: a Google account with your YouTube channel, a free Apify account for TikTok, your email tool.

## Later

- **update my Chat Manager**: after downloading a new version from My Account → Downloads on theambitiondepartment.com.
- **check my Chat Manager**: tests every part and says what needs you.
- **draft my comment replies**: Claude writes reply drafts on your plan; you approve them in your dashboard.

Your keys and your server's key live only in the `.secrets` folder here. Keep a copy somewhere safe.

## Who can use it

You can set Chat Manager up for your own accounts and for every client you manage: one copy per Instagram account.
Please don't resell the kit or give it to someone else to set up for themselves.
