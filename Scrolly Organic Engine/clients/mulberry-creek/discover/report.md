## Your Organic Search Opportunity

**Potential relevant search market:** Broad and multi-category — confirmed by a manual keyword-discovery pass that surfaced 6,477 unique commercial and informational keyword phrases across your nursery/retail and landscaping lines (species-level tree/plant terms, purchase-intent "for sale" phrasing, landscape design/installation sub-intents, and regional North/East Texas informational queries). **We cannot yet turn this into one addressable-market number** — the manual export returns Google's coarse volume buckets with no assigned geography, and doing so would create false precision. **Estimated current organic visibility/capture:** Confirmed thin and uneven. You hold local-pack **#1 for "landscape design" and #2 for "landscape installation" — but only at Wills Point.** You are absent from local pack for "plant nursery" and "landscaping company" at Wills Point, and absent from all four core terms in every other market tested (Terrell, Forney, Emory, Mineola). **Estimated uncaptured opportunity:** Large in scale, unmeasured in precise size. The clearest evidence: your core retail identity (nursery/garden center) has *zero* current local-pack visibility anywhere tested, despite being the larger, described part of a 43-acre operation. A precise current-vs-potential traffic figure requires the O6 ranked-keywords baseline call, which is Pending (funding-blocked).

## Should I Invest in Organic Search?

**Verdict: SELECTIVE GO.**

Not a blanket "invest everywhere" — organic difficulty is unmeasured for nearly every newly-discovered cluster, and several candidate categories (hardscape, most tree species, palms) remain unconfirmed offerings. Not a "weak/no-go" either — you have a *real, already-proven* local-pack win (Landscape Design #1, Installation #2 at Wills Point) sitting next to a *real, already-confirmed* gap on your own core retail identity, and this round's research confirms the relevant keyword universe behind that gap is broad and genuine, not just the 5 head terms originally tested.

**Recommended scope for this Selective Go:** prioritize the Nursery/Garden Center core page and the Landscape Design/Installation expansion — both are confirmed offerings, both are tied to evidence already on file (a documented gap or a documented win). Treat species pages, hardscape, and palm trees as gated behind inventory confirmation and organic-difficulty validation before committing build effort to them.
