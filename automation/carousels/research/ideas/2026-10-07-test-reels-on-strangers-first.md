## The insight
Instagram's trial Reels show a new Reel only to people who don't follow you, give you its views, likes, comments and shares within about 24 hours, and let you share it to everyone (or automatically) if it does well; Instagram says trials are ranked separately and don't affect your regular Reels.

## Why it's worth a carousel
- Who it matters to: people who want more views from new people without testing on their followers.
- What it helps them do: test hooks and formats safely, then share the winners.
- What's interesting: an official, low-risk way to experiment; the automatic "share to everyone" option.
- 5+ slides: what a trial Reel is → who sees it → what you learn in 24 hours → share to everyone (or automatically) → doesn't affect your other Reels → one rule: make it a new Reel (reposts can get limited reach) → how to turn it on.
- Visual angle: phone screen with the "Trial" toggle; a before/after of who sees it.
- Consistent with her method: yes, the Playbook teaches trial Reels (views from non-followers feed the $1K plan).
