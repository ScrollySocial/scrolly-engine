# Recommended starter rules

Rules we learned make Reels look polished and less AI-made. Every one is optional: setup shows them to you a group at a
time, and the ones you keep go into your rules list (`rules.list` in `reel-settings.json`). Claude follows that list
on every Reel. Change it any time: "add a rule", "drop a rule", "show me the starter rules again".

Your colors, fonts and voice are never part of these: setup asks for yours.

## Look

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Graphics go around my body, above my head first, never over my face | Viewers watch your face; covering it loses them |
| Graphics over my body only for emphasis | Keeps the frame clean so the important moment stands out |
| Captions and text never sit over my face | Same reason, and Instagram's own buttons already cover the edges |
| Circles and scribbles never cross the words they mark | The mark should point at the words, not hide them |
| No small label cards above headings ("eyebrows") | One of the clearest signs of an AI-made design |
| Real-looking objects and screens (chat apps, files, receipts, real posts), never drawn stand-ins | Real things read as proof; drawn ones read as clip art |
| Example text is specific to me (a "bad caption" example uses a pattern from my own never-say list) | Generic examples feel fake |
| No em dashes and no "\|" on screen | Both read as AI-written |
| Statements in my big font, key words on the highlighter swipe, side notes handwritten with drawn arrows | A clear, consistent look viewers start to recognise |
| Never fake proof: no invented numbers, results or testimonials | Protects your credibility (and it's often against platform rules) |

## Sound

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Different sounds can stack, but never the same sound twice in a row | Repeats sound like a glitch |
| When I list things, every item can get the same sound | The items feel like a set |
| One music track per Reel, one volume level, with a slow fade in | Easy to adjust on the review page, and never fights your voice |

## Planning and review

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Show me options as pictures or drafts, not descriptions | Video choices are hard to judge from text |
| Show the rough cut as plain lines for my OK before building | Fixing the cut early saves a full render |
| Every Reel has one purpose (Follow, Share, Save, Link in bio…) and the ending is built for it | A Reel that asks for nothing gets nothing |
| Never hand over a draft render as the finished Reel | Drafts are choppy on purpose |

## Covers

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Talking Reels get a custom cover: my cut-out on a flat brand color, the title in the Reel's own fonts | Covers sell the click on your grid |
| Cover text sits in the middle band the profile grid shows | Instagram crops the top and bottom on the grid |
| Cover text is the viewer's problem in their words, never my internal names | Viewers don't know your series names |
| Offer me 5 or 6 cover options before building one | Easier to pick than to describe |
| No solid bands, boxes or cards on covers | They look like ads and crowd the photo |
| Neighbouring posts on my grid never share a cover style (only when a tracker with post dates is connected) | A varied grid looks intentional |
| Trial Reel versions of one Reel can share a cover | They go to non-followers first, so only one lands on your grid |

## Captions and words

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Never "It's X, not Y", "isn't X, it's Y" or contrast slogans | The most recognisable AI writing pattern |
| No patronising filler and no stating the obvious | Respects the viewer's time |
| No fake urgency or "secret", "hack", "game changer" | Reads as spam |
| Never invent statistics, quotes, results or customer stories | Credibility, and often platform rules |
| The ask (follow, link, save) comes in the caption's first two sentences | Most people only read the first lines |
| Exactly 3 hashtags, specific to the post | Enough to categorise the post without looking spammy |
