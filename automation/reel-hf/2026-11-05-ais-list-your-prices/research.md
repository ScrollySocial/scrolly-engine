# Research record: "List your prices on your website" (checked 2026-11-05)

Claim to check: AI assistants favour pages that list prices over "contact us for pricing".

## What holds up
- **Pricing pages get cited more by AI answers.** Discovered Labs, "What actually drives AI citations"
  (https://discoveredlabs.com/research/what-drives-ai-citations, last updated August 2026): ~2 million citations over
  six months across ChatGPT, Claude, Google AI and Gemini; 10,000 cited pages. In their controlled model, pricing pages
  carry a positive coefficient (+0.39) after controlling for content depth, query match and page age; listicle reviews
  come out negative (-0.12). Vendor research (they sell AI-search services), associations only, no confidence interval
  for the pricing figure. It does NOT test "contact us for pricing" pages.
- **ChatGPT shopping uses price as an input.** OpenAI Help Center, Shopping in ChatGPT
  (https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11128490): products are chosen using structured metadata such as price and
  description; merchants are ranked by availability, price, quality and whether they make or mainly sell the item.
  Page returned 403 to a direct fetch; read via search-result copies of the article. Applies to product shopping, not
  service businesses.

## What does NOT hold up
- "AI is going to skip your business every time": no study found that tests hidden pricing against listed pricing, and
  recommendations vary a lot run to run (a BrightLocal analysis cited in guides found repeat local prompts return only
  ~23% of the same businesses; not read in the original). Her spoken words stay as filmed (brief: don't change them),
  but the caption and on-screen graphics don't repeat "every time" or claim a measured skip rate.
- Marketing blogs (themediacaptain.com, brihaspatitech.com, HubSpot) state it as advice or belief, not measured.

## Interpretation used in the caption (not proven by the research)
When someone asks an AI tool what something costs, it can only answer with prices it can read on a page, so a page with
real prices gives it something to quote.
