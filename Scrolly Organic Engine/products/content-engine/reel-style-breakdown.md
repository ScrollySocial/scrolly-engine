# Reel style breakdown: Mercedes's two favorites (2026-10-12)

Source: her screen recordings `OneDrive/Scrolly Media/videos/Sample reel 1.mp4` (@li.gorbunova, ManyChat flow,
86s) and `Sample reel 2.mp4` (@justyn.ai, "I didn't edit this", 59s). Recordings have no audio; this covers visuals only.

## Sample 1: @li.gorbunova (the one she's loved for a while)
**Filming:** one take, one static wide shot (sitting on her bed, laptop on lap), plus a short hands-on-keyboard b-roll.
All the richness is in the graphics layer, so this is achievable from a single talking-head take.
- **Change rate:** about 52 visual changes in 86s (one every 1.6s). The camera never moves; the graphics do.
- **Wardrobe = palette:** magenta striped outfit, yellow pom-pom pen; the graphics reuse magenta, red, yellow, chrome.
- **Captions move around:** usually above her head, sometimes chest height. Two-part lines: a small plain sans
  lead-in ("and in this video") + a big italic serif key phrase ("I'm going to ungatekeep"); key words in yellow.
- **Stickers:** chrome 3D stars, red and black starbursts, deep-pink sticky notes with handwriting
  ("found my buyers", "grew my audience"), the ManyChat logo.
- **Proof cards:** real screenshots of the thing she's teaching (DM flows, a quiz, profile stats, a phone mockup)
  pop in beside her.
- **Background swaps:** a few beats replace the room with a flat color (pale blue, pink with stars, red stripes)
  with her cut out or in a circle mask.
- **Treatments:** a black-and-white beat, motion-blur whip transitions, a circle mask.
- **CTA:** "comment Empire below" + logo.

## Sample 2: @justyn.ai (the high-production one)
**Filming:** several locations and angles (two rooms, wide shot, close-up, a few outfits), plus screen captures.
- **Change rate:** a new visual roughly every 1 to 2s; bursts of rapid-fire cuts (10 in one second around 28s).
- **One tight brand system:** deep forest green + cream paper + a high-contrast serif + small monospace labels
  ("INSTRUCTION 01", "EDIT MODE", timecodes). Feels like a film studio's paperwork. The color grade leans into the green.
- **Captions:** small and quiet, one short line in a dark-green tag at chest height, key word in cream italic serif.
  The big type is saved for moments.
- **Section cards:** full-screen green title cards ("A SINGLE SECOND OF THIS VIDEO", "A FEW MINUTES LATER",
  "and the captions overlay like a movie").
- **Show the process:** paper "instruction" cards, app icons, a contact-sheet grid of footage, film-strip borders,
  a storyboard collage, a phone mockup, a chat bubble, flying keyboard keys, a color-palette picker, an animated
  cursor clicking things. Every claim gets a visual.
- **Kinetic type:** huge serif words behind his head ("NEVER", depth cut-out) and over him ("I DIDN'T EDIT").
- **Treatments:** black-and-white beats, a hand-drawn red circle around him, motion-blur whips, zooms, a wider shot
  for contrast.
- **CTA:** a 'Comment "EDIT"' card plus an animated Follow button.

## What both have in common (the recipe)
1. **A fixed palette that matches the outfit and set.** The graphics feel designed, not added.
2. **Two type voices:** a plain sans for the small words and an italic serif for the key phrase.
3. **Text placed by meaning, not stuck at the bottom.** Often above the head.
4. **Something changes every 1 to 2 seconds,** but there's one focal point at a time.
5. **Every claim gets a visual:** a screenshot, a UI card, a sticker, a logo.
6. **A few signature moves used sparingly:** a black-and-white beat, a background swap, a circle highlight, depth text.
7. **A CTA card designed like the rest of the Reel.**

## What this means for Scrolly
- Sample 1's approach fits how Mercedes films (one take, one spot) and gets most of the effect.
- Sample 2's level needs more filming (angles, locations) and many custom UI animations; save it for hero Reels.
- Scrolly already has the ingredients: cream, peach and orange palette, Instrument Serif italic + a plain sans,
  sparkle and star stickers from the carousels, orange wardrobe.
- She'd supply per Reel: the talking-head take, plus screenshots of whatever she's talking about (Notion Brain,
  calculator, Challenge pages).
