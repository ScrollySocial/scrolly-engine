YouTube is a search engine as much as a video platform. That makes it genuinely different from TikTok and Instagram Reels, even though Shorts looks similar on the surface.

Search vs. recommendation — and why it matters

Long-form video is discovered two ways: someone searches a question, or YouTube recommends it based on watch history. Both reward videos that genuinely answer a real question — a searchable title and a real answer inside matter more here than on TikTok, where the feed cares more about immediate watch-through than search-matching.

"How to clean a cast iron pan" is a real, searchable question with buying intent nearby. "Day in my life as a candle maker" is recommendation-feed content — it can work, but it isn't answering a search.

Shorts vs. long-form

ShortsUnder 60 seconds, discovered mainly through YouTube's Shorts feed — closer to TikTok/Reels distribution mechanics than to long-form search.
Long-formDiscovered through search and "up next" — rewards depth and real answers, not just a strong first 2 seconds.

Worked example: turning a buyer question into a video

This is the method — not a personalized idea generator. (Fresh, personalized video ideas are part of the $1K Challenge, not this module.)

1. Start with a real question a buyer already asks you — "Will this candle scent be too strong for a small bedroom?"
2. Turn it into a searchable title — "Best Candle Scent Strength for Small Rooms (What Actually Works)"
3. Actually answer it in the video — not a teaser that never resolves
4. Give the interested viewer a next step — a specific product link in the description, not just "check out my channel"

How an interested viewer actually reaches you

  • A link in the video description — the most reliable, always-available path
  • A pinned comment restating the link
  • An end screen — useful for depth, not for an external purchase
  • The Shopping tab / product links, where your channel is eligible

If YouTube supplied your $1,000

Views and watch time are not visits — only count people who actually clicked through to your link.

What to watch

High views, low click-through to your linkCheck whether the description and pinned comment actually make the next step obvious.
Clicks arriving, low conversionCheck the landing page, not the video.
Search traffic growing slowlyNormal for long-form SEO-style content — it compounds over months, not days.
