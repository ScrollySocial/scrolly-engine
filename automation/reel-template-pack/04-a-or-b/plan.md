# #4 A or B?: plan (draft, 2026-10-17)

Replaces the batch-1 version (sky-blue A/B, drawn in code). Same way of working as #2 and #3: real photographed props
plus a playful layer, its own world, one storyboard round before animating.

**Idea from the pack list:** sellers with two versions (colours, scents, finishes, sizes). Five clips: both side by
side, A in use, B in use, both again, a hand picking one up. About 12 s.

**Business job:** the Reel asks the viewer to pick, and the pick is the comment. "Comment A or B" is a vote (comments
push the Reel) and the ManyChat keyword at once, so every voter gets the link in their DMs.

## Concept: the eye exam, "better... A? or B?"

Everyone knows the optometrist's line, "which is better, one or two?". The template acts it out: the viewer looks at
the two versions through a real phoropter (the eye-exam mask with two eyepieces), lenses click between A and B, and the
eye chart is the CTA.

World: an eye-exam room. Real phoropter (black and chrome), a real trial lens (round lens in a metal ring), a real
paper eye chart. Colours from the props: chart black on off-white, chrome, and the red and green of the duochrome test.
Big words set like eye-chart letters (black, heavy, spaced); small words in the chart's tiny bottom-line style.

| Time | Beat | What's on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 2 s | Hook | Clip 1 (both side by side) plays. The phoropter swings down from the top over it; the clip is seen through its two eyepieces. Eye-chart letters below: "WHICH ONE ARE YOU?" then "A OR B?". |
| 2 to 5 s | A | Push into the left eyepiece until its ring fills the screen: clip 2 (A in use) inside the lens edge. Chart letter "A" + "[name + one word]". |
| 5 to 8 s | Click | A real trial lens slides across the frame (the optometrist's click); through it is B, and it grows to full screen: clip 3 (B in use). "or B?" + "[name + one word]". |
| 8 to 10 s | Both | Pull back out to the phoropter: A playing in the left eyepiece, B in the right, a red and a green dot (duochrome) over them. Labels A / B. |
| 10 to 12.5 s | CTA | Clip 5 (hand picking one up) full screen. The eye chart rises from the bottom over the lower half; its rows read big to tiny: "COMMENT" / "A OR B" / "and I'll send you the link". |

Clip 4 (both again) is replaced by the two clips in the two eyepieces, so buyers film 4 clips, not 5 (to confirm).

Layers that act as transitions: the phoropter coming down (hook), the zoom through the eyepiece (into A), the trial
lens wipe (A to B), the pull-back (into both), the chart rising (into the CTA).

## Props needed (Mercedes, ChatGPT or royalty-free; white background, straight-on, no brand name)

1. **Phoropter**, front view, the whole instrument, both eyepieces empty and see-through (no glass glare).
2. **Trial lens**, one round optical lens in a thin metal ring with a short handle tab, front view.
3. **Blank eye chart**, a paper wall chart, straight-on, white with a thin black border, NO letters (the letters are
   editable text so buyers can change the CTA), optionally a red and green strip near the bottom.
