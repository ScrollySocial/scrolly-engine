# Reel Template Packs: physical product pack (draft for approval)

**Status:** Phase 2 draft, revised 2026-10-16 for physical sellers. Waiting for Mercedes's OK. Nothing is built yet.
**What this is:** 25 evergreen CapCut Reel templates for small business owners who sell physical products.
The buyer films their own version of each idea and drops the clips into the template's slots.
**Decided in Phase 1:** CapCut. Three packs of 25 by seller type, built one at a time: physical (this list), then digital,
then service. Shared Halloween and Thanksgiving packs with templates for each seller type. One test template
before the rest. Price TBD. No music in the files: each template names a sound mood, and the guide shows where to find
business-safe sound (Instagram's audio picker on a business account, Meta Sound Collection, Mixkit).

## How every template works

- **1080 × 1920, vertical.** Each template is a row of clip slots with fixed lengths, so the buyer knows exactly how
  long to film each shot. Film every shot a few seconds longer than its slot; the template trims it.
- **[Brackets]** are the buyer's words. Everything in brackets gets replaced.
- **Text stays out of the top and bottom of the frame** (Instagram's buttons and caption cover them) and off faces.
- **The last slot is always the sales CTA:** product name plus where to buy, in the buyer's words. Never "comment".
- **Honesty rules printed in the guide:** real reviews, real questions, real numbers and real dates only. Never show a
  customer's name or address.
- **Spoken hook** is optional: say it to camera, record it as a voiceover, or skip it and let the on-screen hook work
  alone.

## The 25

### 01. One product, three ways
- **For:** anything with more than one use: scarves, mugs, totes, jewelry, home decor.
- **Length:** 14 seconds
- **On-screen hook:** One [product], 3 ways
- **Spoken hook:** "Three ways to use one [product]. Number three is the one people miss."
- **Shots:**
  1. 0 to 2s: the product held up to the camera on a plain background.
  2. 2 to 5s: way one, in use.
  3. 5 to 8s: way two, in use.
  4. 8 to 11s: way three, in use.
  5. 11 to 14s: the product on a table, your hand placing it down. (CTA)
- **On-screen text:** "1. [way one]" · "2. [way two]" · "3. [way three]"
- **CTA slot:** "[Product name]. Shop at [where to buy]."
- **Sound mood:** light, upbeat acoustic.

### 02. Pack an order with me
- **For:** anyone who ships physical products.
- **Length:** 15 seconds
- **On-screen hook:** Pack a [product] order with me
- **Spoken hook:** "This one's going to [state]. Let's pack it."
- **Shots:**
  1. 0 to 2s: empty box or mailer, filmed from above.
  2. 2 to 5s: the product going in.
  3. 5 to 9s: the extras: tissue, a thank-you note, a sticker.
  4. 9 to 12s: sealing it. Keep the shipping label out of frame.
  5. 12 to 15s: the finished package on a stack or by the door. (CTA)
- **On-screen text:** "Wrapped in [tissue/paper]" · "+ a handwritten note" · "Sealed and on its way"
- **CTA slot:** "Want one packed for you? [Where to buy]."
- **Sound mood:** the natural packing sounds up front, soft lo-fi underneath.

### 03. Before and after
- **For:** products that change something you can see: cleaning, skincare, organizers, decor, car care.
- **Length:** 10 seconds
- **On-screen hook:** Before [product]
- **Spoken hook:** "Watch this [surface/space] change in two seconds."
- **Shots:**
  1. 0 to 3s: the "before". End the clip by covering the lens with your hand.
  2. 3 to 7s: start the next clip with your hand on the lens and pull it away to reveal the "after".
  3. 7 to 10s: the product in your hand next to the result. (CTA)
- **On-screen text:** "Before" · "After [real time it took]"
- **CTA slot:** "[Product name]. Find it at [where to buy]."
- **Rule:** same light and angle for both shots, no filter on the "after" only.
- **Sound mood:** a beat that drops right at the reveal.

### 04. A or B?
- **For:** sellers with two versions: colors, scents, finishes, sizes.
- **Length:** 12 seconds
- **On-screen hook:** Which one are you: A or B?
- **Spoken hook:** "Two versions, two very different moods. Which one's yours?"
- **Shots:**
  1. 0 to 2s: both side by side.
  2. 2 to 5s: version A in use, close.
  3. 5 to 8s: version B in use, close.
  4. 8 to 10s: both side by side again.
  5. 10 to 12s: your hand picking one up. (CTA)
- **On-screen text:** "A: [name + one word]" · "B: [name + one word]"
- **CTA slot:** "Both at [where to buy]."
- **Sound mood:** playful, bouncy.

### 05. Details you'd miss in a photo
- **For:** handmade and quality-focused products.
- **Length:** 12 seconds
- **On-screen hook:** Details you'd miss in a photo
- **Spoken hook:** "Product photos don't show these, so here they are up close."
- **Shots:**
  1. to 4. 2.5s each: four close-ups, filmed as close as your phone will focus (stitching, texture, label, finish).
  5. 10 to 12s: the whole product, pulled back. (CTA)
- **On-screen text:** one short line per detail, for example "Hand-stitched edges".
- **CTA slot:** "[Product name]. [Where to buy]."
- **Sound mood:** soft piano or ambient.

### 06. Start to finish
- **For:** makers.
- **Length:** 15 seconds
- **On-screen hook:** Making one [product], start to finish
- **Spoken hook:** "Here's every step of one [product], start to finish."
- **Shots:**
  1. 0 to 2s: the raw materials.
  2. 2 to 5s: step one (speed it up in CapCut if it's long).
  3. 5 to 8s: step two.
  4. 8 to 11s: step three.
  5. 11 to 13s: the finished product reveal.
  6. 13 to 15s: the product in use or packaged. (CTA)
- **On-screen text:** "Step 1: [what]" · "Step 2: [what]" · "Step 3: [what]" · "Done"
- **CTA slot:** "Made by hand in [town]. [Where to buy]."
- **Sound mood:** a rhythmic build.

### 07. How big is it really?
- **For:** products where size is hard to judge in photos: jewelry, bags, prints, candles, stickers.
- **Length:** 10 seconds
- **On-screen hook:** How big is it really?
- **Spoken hook:** "Size is hard to tell in photos, so here it is next to things you own."
- **Shots:**
  1. 0 to 2s: the product alone.
  2. 2 to 4s: in your hand.
  3. 4 to 6s: next to a phone or a mug.
  4. 6 to 8s: worn, used or on a shelf.
  5. 8 to 10s: the product on a ruler or tape measure. (CTA)
- **On-screen text:** "In my hand" · "Next to a phone" · "[Exact size]"
- **CTA slot:** "[Exact size]. [Where to buy]."
- **Sound mood:** light and simple.

### 08. A real customer question
- **For:** anyone who gets the same question in DMs, emails or reviews.
- **Length:** 15 seconds
- **On-screen hook:** a screenshot or text bubble of [a real question a customer asked], name hidden.
- **Spoken hook:** "Someone asked me this, and it's easier to show than tell."
- **Shots:**
  1. 0 to 3s: the product, with the question on screen.
  2. 3 to 7s: showing the answer, part one.
  3. 7 to 11s: showing the answer, part two.
  4. 11 to 15s: the product in use. (CTA)
- **On-screen text:** the answer in one or two short lines.
- **CTA slot:** "[Product name]. [Where to buy]."
- **Rule:** real questions only.
- **Sound mood:** chill.

### 09. Who it's for
- **For:** products with a clear buyer.
- **Length:** 12 seconds
- **On-screen hook:** If you [habit or situation], this is for you
- **Spoken hook:** same as the on-screen hook.
- **Shots:**
  1. 0 to 2s: the product.
  2. 2 to 5s: moment one, the product in use by that kind of person (hands only is fine).
  3. 5 to 8s: moment two.
  4. 8 to 10s: moment three.
  5. 10 to 12s: the product on its own. (CTA)
- **On-screen text:** "For the [type one]" · "For the [type two]" · "For the [type three]", for example "For the 6am coffee crowd".
- **CTA slot:** "Sound like you? [Where to buy]."
- **Sound mood:** upbeat.

### 10. Three people to gift it to

### 12. Your order just arrived

### 13. How to use it in 3 steps

### 14. The annoying moment
- **For:** products that fix an everyday annoyance.
- **Length:** 10 seconds
- **On-screen hook:** [The annoying moment, in your customer's words], for example "When your earrings tangle in your bag again"
- **Spoken hook:** "If this happens to you too, watch the second half."
- **Shots:**
  1. 0 to 4s: the annoying moment, acted out (hands only works).
  2. 4 to 8s: the same moment with your product.
  3. 8 to 10s: the product on its own. (CTA)
- **On-screen text:** the annoyance · "Fixed"
- **CTA slot:** "[Product name]. [Where to buy]."
- **Sound mood:** a sound that switches mood at the 4-second mark.

### 15. New drop reveal
- **For:** a new product or a restock.
- **Length:** 12 seconds
- **On-screen hook:** New: [product name]
- **Spoken hook:** "This is the newest one, and you're seeing it first."
- **Shots:**
  1. 0 to 2s: the product covered, boxed or out of focus.
  2. 2 to 5s: the reveal.
  3. 5 to 7s: close-up one.
  4. 7 to 10s: close-up two.
  5. 10 to 12s: the product in use. (CTA)
- **On-screen text:** "[Product name]" · "Out [real day and date]"
- **CTA slot:** "Out [day]. [Where to buy]."
- **Rule:** real dates only.
- **Sound mood:** build, then drop at the reveal.

### 16. What it feels like
- **For:** products people want to touch before they buy: knits, candles, soaps, ceramics, leather, slime, bedding.
- **Length:** 12 seconds
- **On-screen hook:** What [product] feels like
- **Spoken hook:** "You can't touch it through a screen, so here's the next best thing."
- **Shots:** film close, with the sound on.
  1. 0 to 3s: a squeeze, press or stretch.
  2. 3 to 6s: a hand running across the surface.
  3. 6 to 9s: the most satisfying moment (a pour, a fold, a tap, a drape).
  4. 9 to 12s: the whole product, pulled back. (CTA)
- **On-screen text:** one word per shot, in your own words, for example "Soft" · "Heavy" · "Smooth".
- **CTA slot:** "[Product name]. [Where to buy]."
- **Sound mood:** the product's own sound up front, very quiet music or none.

### 17. Everything that goes into one
- **For:** handmade and small-batch products.
- **Length:** 12 seconds
- **On-screen hook:** Everything that goes into one [product]
- **Spoken hook:** "Here's everything that goes into one of these."
- **Shots:**
  1. 0 to 3s: an empty table, filmed from above.
  2. 3 to 8s: your hands placing each material, one at a time (one clip, or speed it up).
  3. 8 to 10s: the finished product placed in the middle.
  4. 10 to 12s: the finished product, close. (CTA)
- **On-screen text:** a label for each material as it lands.
- **CTA slot:** "[Product name]. [Where to buy]."
- **Sound mood:** steady and rhythmic.

### 18. Check this before you buy
- **For:** sellers who know their category well.
- **Length:** 15 seconds
- **On-screen hook:** 3 things to check before you buy a [product type]
- **Spoken hook:** same as the on-screen hook.
- **Shots:**
  1. 0 to 2s: the product type (yours).
  2. 2 to 6s: check one, shown on your product.
  3. 6 to 10s: check two.
  4. 10 to 13s: check three.
  5. 13 to 15s: your product, passing all three. (CTA)
- **On-screen text:** "1. [check]" · "2. [check]" · "3. [check]"
- **CTA slot:** "Mine checks all three. [Where to buy]."
- **Rule:** helpful checks only, never another brand by name.
- **Sound mood:** steady.

### 20. What buyers said
- **For:** anyone with real reviews.
- **Length:** 12 seconds
- **On-screen hook:** What buyers said about [product]

  1. 0 to 3s: the product in use, with review one on screen.

### 21. Small batch
- **For:** makers who make in batches.
- **Length:** 12 seconds
- **On-screen hook:** This week's batch: [real number] [products]
- **Spoken hook:** "I made [number] of these this week. Here they are."
- **Shots:**
  1. 0 to 3s: the whole batch, from above.
  2. 3 to 7s: a slow pass along the batch.
  3. 7 to 10s: one picked up, close.
  4. 10 to 12s: one being packed or held. (CTA)
- **On-screen text:** "[Number] made" · "[One detail about this batch]"
- **CTA slot:** "[Product name]. [Where to buy]."
- **Rule:** the real count only. Never fake "almost gone".
- **Sound mood:** calm.

### 22. Put it to the test
- **For:** products with a quality you can show: waterproof, sturdy, long-lasting, stain-resistant.
- **Length:** 12 seconds
- **On-screen hook:** Does it really [claim]? Let's test it.
- **Spoken hook:** same as the on-screen hook.
- **Shots:**
  1. 0 to 2s: the setup.
  2. 2 to 8s: the test, in one uncut take.
  3. 8 to 10s: the result, close.
  4. 10 to 12s: the product, back in normal use. (CTA)
- **On-screen text:** "[The claim]" · "[What happened]"
- **CTA slot:** "[Product name]. [Where to buy]."
- **Rule:** test only a claim you already make, and show it uncut.
- **Sound mood:** suspense, then a release at the result.

### 23. Every option in 10 seconds
- **For:** products with colors, scents, sizes or designs.
- **Length:** 12 seconds
- **On-screen hook:** Every [color/scent/design] in 10 seconds
- **Spoken hook:** "Every option, fast. Pause on your favorite."
- **Shots:**
  1. to 8. 1.25s each: one option per clip, same framing every time.
  9. 10 to 12s: all of them together. (CTA)
- **On-screen text:** the name of each option.
- **CTA slot:** "All [number] at [where to buy]."
- **Sound mood:** a fast beat, so the cuts land on it.

### 25. Mine vs. the usual kind
- **For:** products that differ from the common version.
- **Length:** 12 seconds
- **On-screen hook:** [Your product] vs. the usual kind
- **Spoken hook:** "Same thing, made two ways. Here's the difference."
- **Shots:**
  1. 0 to 2s: both side by side.
  2. 2 to 5s: difference one.
  3. 5 to 8s: difference two.
  4. 8 to 10s: difference three.
  5. 10 to 12s: yours, in use. (CTA)
- **On-screen text:** "[Difference one]" · "[Difference two]" · "[Difference three]"
- **CTA slot:** "[Product name]. [Where to buy]."
- **Rule:** compare with a generic one you own. Never name or mock another brand, and only show real differences.
- **Sound mood:** playful.
