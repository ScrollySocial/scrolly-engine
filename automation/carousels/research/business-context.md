# Business context for research (Scrolly)

Input for idea research. Facts here come from Mercedes or the live site; pillars are a proposal until she confirms.

- **Audience:** people who want to make their first $1K online, many starting out (Mercedes, 2026-10-06). Do not frame content around audience size (content-rules 1c).
- **Selling now:** $1K Club ($40) and $1K Club + Challenge ($75). Inclusions: `config/destinations.json` (from the live page).
- **Coming soon (no CTAs until live):** Business Brain; Pinterest pin generator (may launch first).
- **Method to stay consistent with (check against it; it is not the only source of content):** the $1K Club Playbook, live edition `Scrolly Organic Engine/products/1k-club/playbook/edition-2/src/playbook3/`. Key frame: $1,000 → sales → website visitors → Instagram views (ch. 6). Sources the Playbook already verified: `refs.mjs` in that folder (re-check dates before reuse).
- **THE NICHE IS ORGANIC MARKETING** (Mercedes, 2026-10-07): helping people get their first $1K of sales through organic content (no paid ads). Every idea must help the reader get found, chosen, or bought from through organic content. Not taxes, fees, accounting, legal, or general small-business admin, even if useful (rejected: "what a $40 sale pays you", "first $1K and taxes").
- **Pillars:**
  1. The $1K plan: working backward from $1,000 to views (her method)
  2. Turning attention into sales: profile, bio link, sales page, the path from post to purchase
  3. Content that gets found and chosen: Reels, carousels, Stories, captions, hooks (Instagram first)
  4. Other organic channels, later: Pinterest and search (when those products launch)
- **Good research sources for this niche:** platform announcements and help docs (Instagram, Meta, Pinterest, Google), large post-level studies with disclosed methods (e.g. Socialinsider, Buffer, Hootsuite, Rival IQ benchmarks), Pew/official data on platform use. The Playbook already cites several (refs.mjs); re-verify before reuse.
- **Research is about the subject, not Instagram creators:** look off-app (studies, official docs, industry news) for ideas; never copy what other accounts post.
