1. Unimpressed stare + "me after…" complaint

WHAT YOU ACTUALLY DO: Film a few seconds of yourself at work looking unimpressed at the camera, and put one line on screen about something that happens to you all the time.

WHAT MAKES IT A TREND: People are all using this same audio under a deadpan look plus a one-line complaint ("Me today after the 5th car in a row tries to modify their unicorn frap").

WHY IT'S BROADLY USEFUL: Every business has a repeat annoyance (the same question, the same request, the same mess), so the line adapts to any job.
