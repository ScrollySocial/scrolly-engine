# Filming day: every shot, both packs

One list for the Halloween pack and the Product pack, for filming with a helper and testing the installer.
Made 2026-10-22 from each template's `1 Put your clips here/READ ME.txt` (the same lines buyers get in
`Shot list - what to film.txt`). If a template's list changes, that READ ME is the truth.

**ME** = you on camera, so your helper films. Times are minimums: film a few seconds longer.

## How to film and test each one

1. Phone upright, near a window, leaned on something so it stays still. Product in the middle of the frame.
2. Film the clips for one template, in order.
3. Rename them 1, 2, 3... and put them in that template's `1 Put your clips here` folder.
4. Double-click `2 Double-click to install`, open CapCut, check it. Tick the box if it looks right; write what
   broke if not.

Do Halloween first: it has to be posted before the 31st.
