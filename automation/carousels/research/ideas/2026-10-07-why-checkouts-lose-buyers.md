## The insight
Among US online shoppers who left a checkout for reasons other than "just browsing", the top reason was surprise extra costs (40%), and several of the next ones are things a small seller controls: forced account creation, a long checkout, not seeing the total up front.

## Why it's worth a carousel
- Who it matters to: anyone sending Instagram traffic to a sales page (her whole method ends at "visitors who buy").
- What it helps them do: check their own checkout against the most common fixable reasons.
- What's interesting: the biggest reasons are surprises and friction, not just price.
- 5+ slides: one reason per slide (extra costs 40%, forced account 18%, long checkout 17%, couldn't see total 12%, not enough payment methods 9%) → what to check on your own page.
- Visual angle: a mock checkout with each problem circled (style E).
- Consistent with her method: yes, ch. 6 says the most useful early work is making the path easier ("a clearer page").
