Why these numbers matter

Before you spend, know how many sales it takes to get the money back. Then you can judge whether the spend is realistic.

Tips to improve your results

  1. Count only the costs that scale with each sale as cost per sale.
  2. Set a date by which you expect to break even.
  3. A higher price shortens the road to break even.
