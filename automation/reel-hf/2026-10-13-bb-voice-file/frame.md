# frame.md — Scrolly "Studio" Reel look (v1, from approved storyboard v4)

Brand truth for this Reel. Source: Scrolly brand (vault brand/visual-system.md) + Mercedes's storyboard notes 2026-10-13.

## Canvas
- 1080×1920, 30 fps. Her talking-head footage is the base layer almost the whole time.
- Her face sits in the upper-middle (roughly y 520–1000). Graphics go AROUND her body: above her head first (y 60–520), then the sides. Never over her face. Over her body only for deliberate emphasis.
- Relaxed captions: centered, top at y ≈ 1380, max 2 lines.
- A soft top fade (black 60% → 0 by ~40–60% height) sits behind anything placed above her head.

## Palette (hex, by role)
- cream #F6EEE5, cream-2 #FFF4DC (paper, light text)
- peach #FFC9A8 (marker swipe, accents)
- ink #161210 and black as neutrals
- orange #E05A33 (rare splash: the ✗, "nope.", banned-line markers, the HELLO badge)
- blush #F8DCE5, mist #E7EEF5 (background-swap panels)
- UI colors are the apps' own: chat bubbles #2F2F2F / #FFFFFF; markdown editor #1F1D1B with green #7FCF93 added-line marks.

## Type (local woff2 in assets/fonts)
- **Statements:** Bricolage Grotesque 800, condensed (font-stretch 78%), sentence case or caps, white (or ink on light panels). Emphasis = a peach marker swipe behind the word (skewed block); the word turns ink. **Never italic, never a color-changed word.**
- **Asides:** Covered By Your Grace (handwritten), cream-2, with a soft dark glow; drawn arrows and circles in the same stroke.
- **Captions:** Figtree 800, white, dark glow shadow.
- **UI:** system UI stack for chat, Consolas/Cascadia Mono for the markdown file.

## Do
- Real objects: a HELLO name badge, manila folders, lined paper, a receipt, her real carousel covers, a real-looking chat, a real `voice.md` file being edited, the real Business Brain design.
- Circles and scribbles go AROUND what they mark.
- One focal element at a time; something changes every 1–2 s.

## Don't
- Italic serif accent words, color-changed words, eyebrow labels over italic titles (reads as AI-made).
- Text or cards over her face.
- Price of the Business Brain.
- Em dashes or "|" in on-screen text. Glow effects, confetti, generic AI imagery.
