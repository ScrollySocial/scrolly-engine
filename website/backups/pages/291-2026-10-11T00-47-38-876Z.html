Is $1,000 guaranteed?

No, and that’s the point. It isn’t a guarantee. It’s a realistic plan built to give you a real shot at it, and the win means something because it isn’t handed to you. Results depend on your offer, your customers and the work you put in.

What counts toward the $1,000?

Total money your business collects for its products or services during your 30 consecutive days. Sales tax, shipping you charge and refunds don’t count. It doesn’t have to be one product.

How do I earn the shirt?

Hit $1,000 in total revenue inside your 30 consecutive days, then show your sales records. Once they’re verified, your stripe shirt ships to you. Everyone who hits the goal earns one.

Is shipping free?

Free to addresses in the lower 48 US states. Anywhere else, you can still earn your shirt: we’ll email you to arrange shipping, which you pay.

What if I don’t hit $1,000 in 30 days?

Can I buy the Challenge on its own?

No. The Challenge is built on the Club, so your first one comes with Club + Challenge.

Can I get a refund?

No. The Club and Challenge are digital products, so all sales are final. See our Refund Policy.
