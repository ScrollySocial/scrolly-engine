Put your clips in this folder, then double-click "2 Double-click to install".

They go into the template in name order, so name them 1 to 4 (for example 1.mov, 2.mov).
Film upright (vertical) and start each clip right on the action.

1: The setup (at least 5 seconds)
2: The test, one take (at least 7 seconds)
3: The result (at least 5 seconds)
4: You, at the end (at least 4 seconds)
