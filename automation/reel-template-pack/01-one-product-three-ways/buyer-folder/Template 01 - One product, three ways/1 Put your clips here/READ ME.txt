Put your clips in this folder, then double-click "2 Double-click to install".

They go into the template in name order, so name them 1, 2, 3, 4 (for example 1.mov, 2.mov).
Film upright (vertical) and start each clip right on the action.

1: Your product, held up or on a table
2: Way one, in use
3: Way two, in use
4: Way three, in use
