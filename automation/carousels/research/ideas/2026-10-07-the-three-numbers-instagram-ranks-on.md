## The insight
Instagram's head, Adam Mosseri, said the top ranking signals are watch time, likes and sends, and told creators to watch average watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach; sends matter slightly more for reaching people who don't follow you yet.

## Why it's worth a carousel
- Who it matters to: anyone trying to get views from new people (her $1K method ends at Instagram views).
- What it helps them do: know which three numbers to check in Insights, and write posts worth sending to a friend.
- What's interesting: it's "per reach", not raw totals; and a send counts for more with strangers than a like.
- 5+ slides: the three signals → watch time → likes per reach (followers) → sends per reach (non-followers) → where to find them in Insights → one practical step (end with a reason to send it).
- Visual angle: an Insights screen with the three numbers circled (style E / D with overlays).
- Consistent with her method: yes, Playbook ch. 8 already teaches this (cites mosseri-signals).
