How to use your Reel templates

Film a few short clips of your product on your phone, drop them into the template, type your own words over the brackets, then post.

1. What you need

  • Your product and your phone camera.
  • CapCut, free. Use the computer version for the project file, or the phone app with the shot guide.
  • The template's recipe card. It lists every clip, how long it is and what to film.

2. Film your clips

  • Hold your phone upright (vertical).
  • Film each shot a few seconds longer than the card says. The template trims it.
  • Stand near a window for soft light, and keep the phone still (lean it on something).
  • Keep your product in the middle of the frame. Words go at the top and bottom.

3a. On a computer

  1. Install CapCut, open it once, then close it.
  2. Copy the template folder (for example Scrolly 01 One product three ways) into CapCut's projects folder. On Windows it's %LOCALAPPDATA%\CapCut\User Data\Projects\com.lveditor.draft. Paste that into the File Explorer address bar.
  3. Open CapCut. The template shows up in your projects.
  4. Click Import and add your clips.
  5. On the timeline, right-click SHOT 1, choose Replace, pick your clip and slide to the part you want. Do the same for every shot.
  6. Double-click each text box and type over the [brackets].
  7. Click Export (1080p, 30 fps).

3b. On your phone

  1. Watch the shot guide video once so you know the timing.
  2. In CapCut, tap New project and add your clips in the order on the card.
  3. Tap each clip and drag its edges until it matches the seconds on the card.
  4. Tap Text, then Add text, and type each line from the card. Drag it to start and end at the times listed.
  5. Tap Export.

4. Add sound in Instagram

The templates come without music, so your Reel never gets muted for a song you don't have rights to.

  • Upload your Reel, then tap Add audio. On a business account, Instagram only shows sounds cleared for business.
  • Each template suggests a mood, like "light, upbeat acoustic". Search for that.
  • Skip CapCut's built-in music unless a track is clearly marked for commercial use.

5. Before you post

  • Watch it once with the sound off. Can you tell what you sell from the words alone?
  • Check the last clip says your product's name and where to buy it.
  • Write a caption whose first line is useful on its own, for example "Three ways to use one tote, including one for the car."
  • Use real reviews, real numbers and real dates only. Never show a customer's name or address.
