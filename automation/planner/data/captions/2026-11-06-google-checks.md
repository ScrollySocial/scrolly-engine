Five free checks that show whether Google can find your website. Each takes a few minutes, so save this for a quiet afternoon.

1. Is Google indexing your pages?
Add your site to Google Search Console (free), then open Indexing, then Pages. It lists the pages Google has indexed and gives a reason for each one it hasn't. Start with your product and service pages: a page that isn't indexed can't show up in search.

2. What does Google show for your site?
Search site:yourwebsite.com on Google. You'll get a rough list of your indexed pages with their titles and descriptions. Look for old pages, test pages and duplicates you forgot about.

3. Do your page titles say what you sell?
The blue link in search results usually comes from your page title. Write it the way a customer would search, then add your business name, for example: Handmade Soy Candles in Austin, TX - Wick and Co. Google often shortens long titles, so put the important words first.

4. How fast does your homepage load on a phone?
Go to pagespeed.web.dev, paste in your homepage and look at the mobile result. Largest Contentful Paint is how long your main image or headline takes to appear, and Google counts 2.5 seconds or less as good. Big photos are a common cause of slow pages, and resizing them is often the quickest fix.

5. Does each thing you sell have its own page?
Someone searching "custom dog portrait" is looking for a page about custom dog portraits. If all your offers share one page, give your main ones their own page, using the words people search for.

Work through these in order. The first one matters most, because a page Google hasn't indexed can't rank at all.
