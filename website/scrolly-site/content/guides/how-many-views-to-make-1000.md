:::note
**The short answer.** The question: "How many views does it take to make $1,000?" 2 or 3 sentences, 40 to 60 words.

Facts to work from:
- $1,000 ÷ $50 = 20 sales.
- 20 sales ÷ 1.4% of visitors buying = 1,429 visitors (rounded up).
- 1,429 visitors ÷ 0.3% of viewers visiting your site = 476,334 views (rounded up).
:::

## The math in three steps

:::flow
Sales | Your goal divided by your price.
Visitors | Your sales divided by the share of visitors who buy.
Views | Your visitors divided by the share of viewers who visit your site.
:::

### Step 1: How many sales $1,000 takes

:::note
Goal ÷ price. Table to keep or drop:

$10 = 100 sales, $25 = 40, $50 = 20, $100 = 10, $200 = 5.

Writing rule: price comes from your costs, margin, similar products and value to the buyer, never from the goal. The
table only shows what each price means for the math.
:::

### Step 2: How many website visitors that takes

:::note
Sales ÷ buy rate. Facts:
- Median online store: about 1.4% of visitors buy (Littledata's Shopify benchmark). The top 20% of stores: 2.6% or more.
- Visitors from organic social buy at about the same rate, 1.42% (ThoughtMetric, Q3 2025).
- 20 ÷ 1.4% = 1,429 visitors.
:::

### Step 3: How many views that takes

:::note
Visitors ÷ visit rate. Facts:
- Nobody publishes one benchmark for "viewers who visit your site", so 0.3% is a judgement call. Say so.
- It's built from two published numbers: Reels send profile visits from roughly 3 to 10% of the people they reach
  (Onimator), and about 1 to 3% of profile visitors tap the link in bio (Hopp, 1.8% median).
- 1,429 ÷ 0.3% = 476,334 views.
:::

## That one percentage changes everything

:::bars Views needed for $1,000, by how many viewers visit your site

:::

:::note
The point to make: going from 0.3% to 1% cuts the views you need by more than two thirds. Your take on what that means
for where to spend effort.
:::

## Ways to get more viewers to your site

:::note
Ideas to pick from (only claim what you've actually seen work):
- A comment word that DMs your link, so nobody has to find your bio (link your ManyChat guide).
- Link stickers in more Stories.
- A clearer hook and a reason to click: say what they get on the other side of the link.
- For the buy rate: a clear, specific offer page, and a bundle or add-on that raises what each order is worth.
:::

## Find your own numbers

:::note
How to swap typical rates for real ones after a few weeks of posting:
1. Views: Instagram Insights, per post. Views count replays, so they run higher than accounts reached.
2. Visit rate: external link taps ÷ views (both in Insights).
3. Buy rate: orders ÷ visitors, from your shop's analytics.

Then point to the free Income Goal Calculator (Weak, Typical and Strong presets for both rates).
:::
