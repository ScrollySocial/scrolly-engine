Put your clips in this folder, then double-click "2 Double-click to install".

They go into the template in name order, so name them 1 to 5 (for example 1.mov, 2.mov).
Film upright (vertical) and start each clip right on the action.

1: Detail 1 (at least 5 seconds)
2: Detail 2 (at least 5 seconds)
3: Detail 3 (at least 5 seconds)
4: Detail 4 (at least 5 seconds)
5: The whole product (at least 4 seconds)
