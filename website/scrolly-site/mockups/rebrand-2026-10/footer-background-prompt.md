# Footer background: image prompt (2026-10-19)

Reference: Mercedes's torn-paper mountain collage set (layered strips with cream deckled edges, paper grain, sun at the top).
Goal: the same collage technique, re-coloured navy, with a Starry Night-style swirling sky, foiled stars at the top, and
quiet space where the footer text and the "Mercedes riding a tiger" cut-out will sit. Layout matches the current mockup
footer: quote on the left, tiger image on the right, menu columns along the bottom.

## Main prompt (desktop, wide)

A handmade torn-paper collage night sky, layered like stacked strips of torn art paper, each strip with a soft cream
deckled paper edge (#f7f3ec) and visible paper grain, slight crinkle texture, like a vintage magazine collage. Palette is
deep ink navy (#0a1930) and midnight blues, with only faint whispers of lavender (#d6c6ff), baby pink (#ffc4dd) and hot
pink (#ff3b7f) inside the swirls. The top edge of the image is a torn cream paper edge, as if the night sky is torn open
beneath a cream page. Across the top third: a scatter of foil-stamped stars in gold and silver leaf, four-point and
eight-point sparkle stars of different sizes, catching light with real metallic foil texture, a few tiny star sparkles
between them. The middle band: a swirling night sky inspired by Van Gogh's Starry Night, done as torn and layered paper,
with sweeping spiral wind currents and glowing halos around a few stars, cut from navy, indigo and faint lavender paper.
Keep the swirls mostly in the upper left and centre. The right half of the middle band stays calm and simple, a smooth
deep navy paper area with only very soft texture, leaving clear empty space for a figure to be added later. The bottom
40 percent is two or three torn layers of very dark navy paper, low detail and low contrast, so white text can sit on it
and stay readable. No people, no animals, no buildings, no text, no lettering, no logos. Surreal, high fashion, editorial,
dreamy, handcrafted, flat lighting, shot straight on like a scanned collage. Wide format.

**Size:** 16:9 (for example 2912 × 1632), or as wide as your tool allows.

**Midjourney add-on (if you use it):** `--ar 16:9 --style raw --v 7`
