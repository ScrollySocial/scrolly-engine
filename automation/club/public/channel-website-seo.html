Search intent: what is someone actually trying to do?

Informational"How do I get candle wax out of carpet" — not ready to buy yet, but might remember you.
Commercial"Best non-toxic candles for allergies" — comparing options, close to a decision.
Transactional"Buy soy candle lavender" — ready to purchase now.

Product/category pages should target transactional and commercial intent; supporting content should target informational intent that links back to what you sell.

AI discovery: what's actually known

AI answer tools (Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT search, Perplexity) synthesize responses partly from indexed web content. Clear, factual, well-structured pages that plainly state what you sell, for whom, and at what price are more likely to be accurately represented — a reasonable inference from how these systems work, not a guaranteed placement.

Be skeptical of specific "AI SEO hack" claims — this space is new and changes quickly, and much confident-sounding advice is speculation. The safest approach is the one that's always worked: be genuinely clear, accurate, and useful.

Answering buyer questions directly

A genuine FAQ section — real questions your customers actually ask, answered plainly — helps both human visitors and search/AI systems understand what you offer and address common objections before checkout.
