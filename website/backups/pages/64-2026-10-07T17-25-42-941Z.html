Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Scrolly SEO collects, why it is needed, and the choices available to you.

Effective date: August 25, 2026

Scrolly SEO is a business operated by a sole proprietor in Texas, United States. This policy applies when you visit this website, submit a form, purchase a workbook, or order an SEO audit.

Information we collect

  • Checkout information: details needed to process and support a purchase. Payment-card information is handled by the payment processor and is not stored directly by Scrolly SEO.
  • Contact and intake information: your name, email address, website, business details, goals, and other information you choose to provide.
  • Optional read-only access: information available through services you voluntarily connect for an audit.
  • Basic website data: standard technical and analytics information about visits and site usage.

How information is used

Information is used to deliver purchases, prepare requested audits, respond to messages, maintain the website, prevent misuse, and improve Scrolly SEO products and methods. Personal information is not sold.

Service providers

Scrolly SEO may use service providers for checkout, forms, email delivery, website hosting, analytics, and SEO research. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention

General inquiries may be retained for up to 24 months after resolution. Audit intake information and completed reports may be retained for up to 24 months after delivery so order questions can be addressed. Transaction records may be kept longer when required for accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, or legal purposes. Information may be deleted sooner when it is no longer reasonably needed.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information through the form below. Some records may need to be retained when required by law or for legitimate transaction and dispute purposes.
