## Corrections to the pasted summary
- Claim 5 is EClinicalMedicine, published December 2024 (pasted as "2025"). Authors call the step gain "trivial" (pasted as "small").
- Claim 1 population is overweight adult employees, mostly men; claims are worded "adults".
- Claim 4 was published online 2019.

## Important qualifications
- All studies are about health, learning or general behaviour. None measured income or sales. Never say the Challenge makes anyone more likely to earn $1,000.
- The 41% vs 25% is share of days, not share of people who reached an overall goal.

## Interpretation (not proven by the research)
- The $1K Challenge has a specific goal ($1,000), a deadline (30 days) and a weekly check-in: these match the parts with the strongest evidence. Worded as "has these parts", never "proven to work".
