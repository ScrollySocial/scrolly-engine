## Day 51 · Nov 4 · card cf8d4dff0e6c

51 days until Christmas, and that's plenty of time to make your first sales online 🎄

When you want extra money for Christmas, start with the number before you think about what to post. Here's how I'd plan $1,000 in sales before the 25th, working backward 👇

The $1K Math, step by step
1. Start with the goal: $1,000 in sales.
2. Divide it by your price. A $25 product takes 40 sales. A $100 service takes 10 clients.
3. Work out the visitors those sales need. That depends on the share of visitors who buy.
4. Then work out the Instagram views that bring those visitors. That depends on the share of views that tap through to your page.
5. You won't know those two percentages until you've posted for a while, so start with an estimate and swap in your real numbers as they come in.

Where your real numbers are
6. On Instagram: Profile → Professional dashboard → Insights.
7. Divide your external link taps by your views for the same week. That's your rough view-to-visit rate.
8. Your shop or booking page shows visits and sales. Sales divided by visits is your buy rate.

What 51 days gives you
9. About seven weeks to post, learn what works and adjust.
10. Time to test a few hooks in the first week and keep the ones that bring visits.
11. Room to fix one weak spot at a time: the offer, the profile or the checkout.

## Day 50 · Nov 5 · card 4e05bbe4611d

Imagine buying the presents this year with money from your own little business 🎁

November and December are when people go looking for gifts, so this is a good season to sell something gift-ready. Here are offers that fit the season, and how to post them 👇

Gift-ready offers
1. Printables and templates: gift tags, party invitations, holiday planners, meal plans.
2. A gift set: two or three things you already sell, bundled with a gift price.
3. Custom orders with a clear cut-off date, like "order by December 12 for Christmas".
4. Gift cards for a service, for the people who shop late.
5. A small starter package for a service, easy to give as a present.
6. A digital guide for a holiday job people dread: hosting, budgeting, wrapping, decorating.

How to post them
7. Reels: show the gift being opened or used, so people picture giving it.
8. Carousels: a gift guide by person, like "for the friend who loves to bake".
9. Stories: the cut-off date, the price and the link, every few days.
10. Your bio: one line with the offer and the order deadline.

Pricing it
11. Set your price from your costs, what similar gifts sell for and what it's worth to the person buying it.
12. Then work out how many sales your $1,000 takes at that price. At $20 that's 50 sales, at $50 it's 20.

What if this year's Christmas present to yourself was finally starting that business? 🎄

You don't need everything figured out to make your first sale. You need one offer, a way to pay and a reason for people to find you. Here's a seven-day start 👇

Day 1: pick one offer
1. Choose the thing you could sell this month, using what you already know how to do.
2. Write one sentence: who it's for and what it helps them do.

Day 2: set the price
3. Add up your costs, look at what similar offers sell for, and think about what it's worth to the buyer.
4. Then divide $1,000 by your price to see how many sales your goal takes.

Day 3: make it easy to buy
5. Set up one checkout or booking link: a shop, a payment link or a booking page.

Day 4: get your profile ready
7. Put what you sell in your name line.
8. In your bio, say who it's for, and point the link straight to the offer.

Day 5: plan your first posts
9. Write down five questions your buyers ask, and turn each one into a post.

Day 6: post your first Reel
10. Show the offer or the result it gives, and say how to get it.

Day 7: share it in Stories
11. Post the link, the price and one reason to buy now.
12. Then keep going, one post at a time.

## Day 45 · Nov 10 · card 6c08961e02d1

Dear Santa, I'd like some customers for Christmas 🎅 Actually, let's make a plan instead.

Customers come from a path: someone sees your post, visits your page and buys. When sales are slow, one step on that path is usually leaking. Here's how to find it 👇

Step 1: people see your posts
1. Check your views in Insights each week.
2. If views are low, test new hooks: the first line on screen and the first second of the Reel.

Step 2: they tap through to your page
3. Divide link taps by views for the same week.
4. If views are fine but taps are low, say what you sell and where to get it, in the post and in Stories.
5. Use a link sticker in Stories, and keep the bio link going straight to the offer.

Step 3: they buy
6. Divide sales by page visits.
7. If people visit but don't buy, look at the page: a clear photo, the price, what's included and one button.
8. Answer the top three questions on the page itself.
9. Add a review or a result as soon as you have one.

Step 4: make the next one easier
10. Thank every buyer and ask what made them decide.
11. Use their words in your next posts.
12. Fix one step a week, so you can see which change helped.

45 days is enough time to find your leak and fix it.
