About Scrolly SEO

Scrolly SEO helps small businesses get found by the customers already searching for what they sell — starting with a clear, evidence-based picture of where they stand today.

What we do

We build two kinds of tools:

  • A Personalized SEO Site Audit — a 29-section, evidence-based analysis of your site's search visibility, technical performance, and growth opportunities, delivered within 2 business days.
  • Guided workbooks — the Shopify SEO Workbook and the Pinterest SEO Workbook — for business owners who'd rather do the research and strategy work themselves.

How we work

Every finding we hand you is labeled by exactly how it was produced, so you always know what's fact and what's judgment:

  • Observed — we analyzed it directly.
  • Retrieved — pulled from real data sources.
  • Calculated — calculated from retrieved data.
  • Inference — our analysis, clearly labeled as judgment.
  • Pending — not yet verified, and clearly marked as such.

We don't invent metrics or dress up guesses as data. If something can't be verified, we say so.
