Why these numbers matter

Your conversion rate shows how well your page and offer turn interest into sales. It is often easier to improve than traffic.

Tips to improve your results

  1. Use the same time period for visitors and orders.
  2. Lead with what the buyer gets, in their words.
  3. Remove steps between “I want this” and paying.
  4. Test one change at a time so you know what worked.
