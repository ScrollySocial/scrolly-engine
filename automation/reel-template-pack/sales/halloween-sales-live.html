Questions

How do I edit the carousel?
Open its Canva link (a free account works), swap the photos and type over the [brackets].
Is music included?
No. The Reels have no audio: no music and no sound effects. Add a sound in Instagram after you upload, so your Reel never gets muted.
Do I need to install fonts?
No, they come with the template.
How do I change the words?
Double-click a text box and type.
My computer warns me about the installer.
That is normal. Click More info, then Run anyway.
Can I swap a clip?
Yes. Right-click it on the timeline and choose Replace.
Do I need Canva Pro?
No. The carousels open in free Canva.
