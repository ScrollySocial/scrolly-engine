Connections

Optional, read-only, disconnect anytime. Connecting a place you sell or market saves you from entering things by hand, but everything here also works fully with zero connections. You can always enter revenue, sales, and visits yourself on Performance.

Three different things can show here, and they mean different levels of trust: Connected means Scrolly is reading real, authorized data directly from that account. Catalog imported means we read your store's public product list, nothing private, and nothing connected to live revenue. Manual means you're telling us the numbers yourself on Performance. A catalog import is never shown as connected revenue.
