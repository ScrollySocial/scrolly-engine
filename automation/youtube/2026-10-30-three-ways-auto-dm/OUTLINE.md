## Before you film (do these first)

1. **Test Meta's free tool on your own account and screen-record it.** Meta Business Suite on desktop → Inbox →
   Automations → Create automation → Comment to message. Write down what really happens:
   - Does it work on your account? Meta's help page says Inbox automations are for "Instagram business accounts"
     connected to Meta Business Suite, and @ambitiondepthq is a Creator account.
   - How long until the DM arrives? Meta's page says *message* keyword replies go out after 15 minutes unless you
     answer first. Check whether comment replies wait the same way.
   - Does "chatterbox" in lowercase trigger a keyword set as "CHATTERBOX"? Meta says message keywords are
     case-sensitive and must match exactly.
   - Can the DM carry a button or a link? Can it collect an email? Can it send a second message?
   - Meta's two help pages describe it differently ("keywords or phrases" on one, "hashtags" on the other). Your test
     settles it.
2. **Use a test-only keyword for Meta's tool (for example FREETEST), never one Chat Manager answers.** Instagram allows
   one private reply per comment, so if both tools answer the same word, one of them fails. Turn the Meta automation
   off after filming.
3. **ManyChat:** only say you used it if you did. Its Free plan needs no card, so you can sign up and record the
   builder if you'd like. Otherwise show the pricing page and keep it to what the page says.
4. **Re-check ManyChat's prices on the day you film** (manychat.com/pricing, Monthly tab).

## 1. The three rules every tool follows (about 2 min)

Screen: Meta's Instagram Private Replies page, the "Limitations" part, highlighted line by line.

1. **One message per comment.** The DM someone gets after commenting is what Meta calls a private reply, and you get
   exactly one.
2. **It has to go out within 7 days** of the comment. For a Live, only while the broadcast is still on.
3. **After that, they have to answer first.** When they reply, you can keep talking for 24 hours from their reply.

> "So the most important part of any setup is that first message. If it only drops a link, the conversation is over.
> If it asks them to reply, you get the 24 hours to send the link, a reminder, or a question."

Show your own DM thread: comment → "reply READY" → link.

## 2. Way 1: Meta's free Comment to message (about 2 to 3 min)

Screen: your test recording in Meta Business Suite, desktop.

- Where it is: Meta Business Suite, desktop only → Inbox → Automations → Create automation → Comment to message.
  Meta describes it as sending "a message reply to comments on your posts that contain specific keywords or phrases".
- What it costs: nothing.
- What you found in your test: setup time, how long the DM took, whether lowercase worked, buttons or links, emails.
  **Fill this in from your recording; say only what you saw.**
- From Meta's help pages: keyword automations go up to 5 keywords or phrases, and keyword replies to *messages* wait
  15 minutes unless you answer first.
- Who it fits: one freebie, a link in a DM, not many comments yet, and no budget.

## 3. Way 2: ManyChat (about 2 min)

Screen: manychat.com/pricing on the Monthly tab, then the plan list.

- ManyChat is an official Meta partner and uses the same Instagram API anyone can use (Under the Hood #3).
- Prices on the Monthly tab, checked 2026-10-30 (re-check on the day):

  | Plan | Monthly | Billed yearly | Active contacts a month |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Free | $0 | $0 | 25 (up to 4 live triggers, ManyChat branding) |
  | Essential | $17 | $14/mo | 250 |
  | Pro | $39 | $29/mo | 2,500 |

- An active contact is one person you message with in a month, however many messages you exchange. Above the limit
  you pay an overage per extra person.
- What the money pays for: the drag-and-drop builder, hosting, support, and never touching a setup screen. It also
  covers TikTok DMs and WhatsApp, which the other two don't.
- Who it fits: you want it working today, you're happy to pay monthly, or you need more channels than Instagram.

> "25 contacts on the free plan goes quickly. If one Reel gets 40 people commenting your word, that's already over."

## 4. Way 3: your own (about 2 min)

Screen: your Chat Manager dashboard, the Flows tab for one keyword.

- Quick recap, then a card to video 1 for the costs and the setup traps.
- How it follows the three rules: the first DM asks them to reply READY. Their reply opens the 24-hour window, so it
  sends the signup link, plus one reminder an hour later if they haven't signed up. Their email goes into the
  keyword's MailerLite group.
- Cost: the $6 a month server. No contact limit.
- What you give up: setup time (a Meta app, a privacy policy page, publishing the app) and fixing it yourself when
  something changes.
- Who it fits: you want the emails and the follow-up without a monthly bill that grows with your comments, and you're
  OK with one afternoon of setup.
