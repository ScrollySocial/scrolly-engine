Branded vs. product/service search

Branded search — someone searching your business name — means they already know you; the job is making sure they land somewhere useful. Product/service search — "non-toxic soy candles" or "emergency plumber near me" — means Google has to decide your business is relevant to a stranger, which depends on your website content, your Business Profile, and (for local/service businesses) proximity and reviews.

Is Google Business Profile relevant to you?

GBP is built for businesses with a physical location customers visit, or that serve customers in a defined area (plumbers, cleaners, local shops, clinics). A purely online business with no service area is generally not eligible the way a local business is — check Google's current eligibility rules for your specific situation before assuming either way.

Google Shopping — if you sell products

Google Merchant Center lets ecommerce businesses list products in Google's free shopping listings (separate from paid ads) — a real, no-cost visibility surface. Setup involves a product feed matching your actual catalog; requirements change over time, so confirm current setup steps directly in Merchant Center.

Using Posts and Q&A

Not a requirement, but a free, visible surface if kept current — an abandoned Posts feed is worse than none at all. Use it for real updates (hours changes, new offerings), not filler.
