# Holiday and seasonal pack ideas (Mercedes, 2026-10-17)

Her own ideas, saved word for word in spirit, for the Halloween, fall and Thanksgiving packs. The vibe to aim for
across all of them: **the pumpkin pie where each slice is taken away to reveal something behind it (circular)**:
real objects, with pieces removed or added to reveal or change what's underneath. Music and sound effects matter a lot.

## Halloween
- The disappearing trick: someone walks, snaps or claps, and their body (or a product) is replaced by a transparent
  smoke video. Could work for a person or a product: "get them before they disappear". May need more teaching to execute.
- The question box set on fire.
- Carved pumpkins.
- Candy.
- Knocking on doors.
- Spiders and cobwebs.
- Ghosts (more cute than scary).
- Candy corn.
- Funny, silly, cute monsters.
- Bats (not too scary).
- Floating lit candles (the Harry Potter kind).
- Music and sound effects are very important.

## Fall
- Pumpkins and gourds.
- Pumpkins that get decorated on screen with paint, jewels, stripes (inspiration:
  https://www.grandinroad.com/harvest-pumpkin-stack/1471540).
- **Pumpkin pie sliced, one slice taken away at a time, revealing something behind the pie (circular).**
- Leaves.
- Apples and apple cider: bobbing apples (real apples animated to bob), with polka-dot or other cute overlays.
- Chai, coffee, lattes.
- Bonfires.
- Plaid.
- Sweaters.
- Hay rides, hay.
- Mums.
- S'mores.

## Thanksgiving
- Turkeys.
- Feathers.
- Cornucopia.
- Rolls.
- Leaves.

- Gingerbread.
