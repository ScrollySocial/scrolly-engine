Accessibility Statement

Scrolly is committed to making its website and digital services usable by as many people as possible.

Our commitment

We are working toward alignment with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA and continue to improve the usability of our pages, forms, documents, and purchasing experience.

Need assistance?

If you experience difficulty using this website, use the form below. Please include the page URL, a description of the problem, and the browser or assistive technology you were using when possible.

Ongoing improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We review reported barriers and use that feedback to improve the experience. This statement does not claim that every part of the site is fully conformant in every technology or circumstance.

If something gets in your way

If something on the site stops you from buying or using a product, tell us and we'll help you complete it another way, for example by email.

Other formats

Need one of our downloads in a different format? Contact us and we'll provide one.

Accessibility toolbar

The accessibility button in the corner lets you adjust text size, contrast and other display settings. It's an optional aid; we still fix issues on the site itself.

Known limitations

  • Some older sales pages (our SEO workbooks and SEO Site Audit) still have colored text and labels that don't meet recommended color contrast. We're updating these.
  • Card payment fields at checkout are provided by our payment processors (Stripe and PayPal), and their accessibility is managed by them.
  • On the checkout page, screen readers may announce the coupon section after the purchase button. Keyboard users reach it in on-screen order.

Accessibility log

  • October 3, 2026: Improved color contrast on product pages (prices, buttons, labels and tabs) and added a clearer keyboard focus outline in the member account area.
  • September 30, 2026: Improved keyboard navigation on checkout, fixed color contrast on the homepage, shop, product listing, checkout and member account pages, and checked the member area for keyboard access, labels and contrast.

Last reviewed: October 8, 2026.
