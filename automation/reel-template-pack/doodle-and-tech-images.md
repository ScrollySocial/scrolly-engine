## Doodle pack

**How to ask ChatGPT:** *"Hand-drawn [doodle] in thick black marker, slightly uneven line with dry-brush edges,
isolated on a pure white background, spread out so no two shapes touch, no text."* Ask for a sheet of 6 to 12
separate shapes per prompt (they get cut apart, so they must not touch). Black only: CapCut can recolour them to any
brand colour.

1. **Arrows:** straight, curved, looping, double-headed, squiggly, a bent "look here" arrow (12).
2. **Bursts:** starbursts, sun rays, "pow" spikes, lines radiating from a point (8).
3. **Speech and thought bubbles:** round, square, spiky "shout" bubble, cloud thought bubble (8).
4. **Framing shapes:** hand-drawn circles, ovals, rectangles, a wobbly box, a scribbled frame (to circle a product) (10).
5. **Underlines:** straight, wavy, double, dotted, a marker swipe (10).
6. **Stars, hearts, crowns, sparkles, lightning bolts** (12).
7. **Faces and eyes:** smileys, a winking face, cartoon eyes, a lips mark (8).
8. **Ticks and crosses:** check marks, X marks, a tick box (8).
9. **Scribbles:** a scribbled-out blob, a tangled scribble, a cross-hatch patch (8). They go next to text, never over it.
10. **Numbers 1 to 5 and $ % + ? !** drawn in thick marker (one sheet).
11. **Squiggles and loops:** spirals, loops, a dashed path with an arrow at the end (8).
12. **Hand-painted polka-dot sheets** (fill the frame): small black dots on cream; big chunky rough dots on cream;
    the same two in white on black. (4 images)
13. **Marker pattern tiles** (fill the frame): stripes, zigzags, checks, crosses, dots, wavy lines (6 images).
14. **Watercolour blobs:** 6 soft round blobs in butter yellow, blush, sage, sky blue, terracotta, lilac, on white.
15. **Watercolour washes** (fill the frame): the same 6 colours as soft backgrounds.
16. **Bold flat florals** (fill the frame): 2 patterns, big modern flowers on a dark navy ground and on a cream
    ground (for fun lettering fills and backgrounds).
17. **Line-art objects** (thin black line, one continuous-line look): a shopping bag, a gift box, a coffee cup, a
    heart, a hand pointing, a lightbulb, a price tag, a shipping box (8).

## Technology pack

**How to ask ChatGPT:** *"Photorealistic studio product photo of [device], [angle], isolated on a pure white
background, soft even lighting, no shadow, no brand logos, the screen showing solid bright green (#00FF00)."* The
green screen lets the buyer's clip go inside the screen exactly. Make every angle of one device in the same chat.
No Apple, Samsung or other logos anywhere.

1. **Laptop:** open, straight on (screen facing camera); open at 3/4 view; closed, from above.
2. **Desktop computer:** monitor with stand, straight on; plus a keyboard and mouse, from above.
3. **Phone:** front, straight on; tilted at 3/4; held in a hand (hand only); back of the phone, plain case.
4. **Tablet:** straight on, landscape and portrait; with a stylus pencil lying beside it.
5. **Earbuds:** the open charging case with both buds, 3/4 view; a single bud.
6. **Over-ear headphones**, 3/4 view.
7. **Smartwatch**, straight on, green screen.
8. **Ring light on a stand** with a phone clipped in, front view.
9. **Phone on a small tripod**, front view.
10. **Camera:** a mirrorless camera, front 3/4 view.
11. **Microphone:** a clip-on lavalier mic; a desk podcast mic on an arm.
12. **Charging cable**, coiled, from above; a power bank.
13. **Desk setup flat lay** (fills the frame): a wooden desk from above with laptop, phone, coffee and a notebook,
    screens green.
14. **Old-school tech for fun:** a cassette tape, a flip phone, a floppy disk, a retro computer monitor (green screen).
