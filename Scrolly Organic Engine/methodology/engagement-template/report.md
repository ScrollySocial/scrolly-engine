# Discover Report — <business name>

Simple customer report ("A" in discover.md's Output section). Derived entirely from `findings.md` — never authored independently. Structured section-for-section per [report-contract.md](../report-contract.md) — every section below is required; where evidence is missing, keep the section and state what was evaluated / confirmed / unknown / what would resolve it (never delete the section, and running out of paid-tool credits is never a reason to). Main report = decisions; the appendix carries the depth — but "concise" governs prose, not itemized findings: Sections 3, 4, 10, and 15 render their full itemized detail here, not a summary standing in for it.

## 1. Executive Opportunity Summary
Biggest opportunity · biggest weakness · strongest existing advantage · best near-term win · highest-value long-term opportunity · overall recommendation (Go / Selective Go / Hold / No-Go) · risks/dependencies.

### SEO Scorecard
Technical SEO · Website architecture · On-page SEO · Organic visibility · Local visibility (if applicable) · GBP/local ecosystem · Keyword opportunity · Content opportunity · Competition · Backlinks/authority · Organic conversion readiness · Analytics/measurement readiness · AI discovery/readiness. Each: a clear client-friendly status, not a numeric score.

## 2. Current Wins
Never omit this section, even in a weak-verdict report. Per win: what's winning · where · which query/page/market · why it matters · how to protect/expand it.

## 3. Technical SEO Analysis
**Full itemized table, not a prose summary** — FIX NOW / IMPROVE / PASSED / NOT EVALUATED. PASSED items stay even when problems were also found. Per issue: issue · severity · evidence · page(s) affected · why it matters · recommended fix · priority.

## 4. Lighthouse / Performance Error Analysis
Per representative page type (homepage, main service/product page, category page, a location page, a blog article, a major landing page, an ecommerce product page where applicable): category scores, Core Web Vitals where available, and the actual failed audits/opportunities/diagnostics — not just the score. Severity (Critical/High/Medium/Low/Informational) and scope (site-wide/template-wide/page-type/individual-page) per issue.

## 5. Analytics & Measurement Readiness
GSC readiness · GA4 readiness · lead/call/form/booking attribution · measurement gaps. State plainly what could and couldn't be verified if access wasn't authorized — never drop this section for that reason.

## 6. Website Structure & Page Architecture
Current structure (what exists, what each page targets) · Recommended structure (Create/Retain/Expand/Rewrite/Consolidate/Redirect/Reposition, with URL/primary keyword/supporting cluster/intent/purpose/geography/priority/existing-page-affected per page) · recommended in-page sections (pricing, FAQs, service areas, trust signals, CTAs, etc. — not just new pages).

## 7. Keyword Opportunity Analysis
Clustered, not a raw list. Confirmed Opportunities and Potential Opportunities / Verify Offering kept visibly separate. Per cluster: primary + supporting keywords · intent · funnel stage · demand · geographic relevance · current visibility · organic difficulty · SERP competition · business value · target page · priority · confidence/source.

## 8. Keyword Competition & Expected Ranking Difficulty
Per major cluster: who ranks, competitor authority/content depth, local competitors, review/backlink/structure gaps · difficulty (Low / Low–Moderate / Moderate / Moderate–High / High / Very High) · range-based realistic ranking-timeframe expectations with assumptions stated. Never a guaranteed date.

## 9. SERP Composition, AI Overviews & Zero-Click Risk
Per high-priority cluster: what the SERP actually looks like (AI Overview / Local Pack / featured snippet / PAA / video / image / shopping / forum-social / blue links) and what that means for expected CTR, ranking strategy, and content structure.

## 10. Competitive Landscape
**Full per-competitor detail, not a few isolated mentions.** Google competitors, not assumed offline competitors — separated by business line where relevant. Why they're winning / how the client can compete, per significant competitor.

## 11. Google Business Profile & Local Search
(Local/regional businesses.) GBP configuration · reviews · NAP/citations · geographic visibility. Market classification: Core / High Priority / Secondary / Expansion / Monitor / Not Recommended. No thin doorway pages. If GBP access is unavailable, keep the section and state exactly what could/couldn't be verified.

## 12. Organic Conversion Readiness
Per major money page: CTA visibility, trust signals, form/booking friction, mobile conversion friction, and other obvious leak points. SEO-ROI framing, not a full CRO pass.

## 13. Blog & Informational Content Opportunity
Size the real opportunity before proposing a cadence. "What to Publish First" — a prioritized ~10–30-article launch list, each with primary keyword, supporting cluster, intent, funnel stage, related money page, demand, difficulty, why it matters, priority.

## 14. Full Content Map
Pointer only — the full 100–400-topic map (when evidence supports that scale) lives in the appendix, never padded. If this round's research was interrupted or narrow, say so explicitly rather than presenting a small count as the true ceiling.

## 15. Backlink & Authority Analysis
**Full classification, not dropped in favor of a busier round elsewhere.** Critical blocker / Meaningful weakness / Secondary opportunity / Healthy for current stage. Realistic authority-building recommendations where appropriate.

## 16. AI Discovery & Readiness
Whether the business appears/is referenced in AI-assisted search for its priority topics, who appears instead, and whether its content/entity signals are clear enough to be cited. Classification: Strong / Moderate / Weak / Not Yet Established. Specific, non-gimmicky improvements only.

## 17. Entity & Business Information Readiness
Consistency of business name/type/services/products/locations/hours/contact/expertise across website, schema, GBP, citations, and social profiles. Identify inconsistencies and missing signals.

## 18. Chatbot / Conversational Opportunity
Classification: High-value / Potential / Low priority / Not recommended, tied to an evidenced business problem (repetitive questions, lead qualification, quote intake, after-hours inquiries, etc.) — never recommended just because AI exists.

## 19. Light AI Automation Opportunity
Brief, secondary — only where it materially affects organic growth or lead conversion (lead routing, follow-up, review-response assistance, reporting).

## 20. Expected Organic Traffic Impact
Current baseline · near-term scenario · mid-term scenario · longer-term implemented-strategy scenario. All CTR/ranking-band assumptions stated. Never sum overlapping keyword volumes as unique visitors.

## 21. Lead / Business Impact Scenarios
Only with client-provided or clearly sourced economics — otherwise traffic/lead scenarios stand alone with `client_specific_economics: Not provided`, never a fabricated revenue forecast. Acknowledge non-form value for local businesses (calls, Maps actions, direction requests, walk-ins, quote requests, branded search).

## 22. Quick Wins
Exact action · why it matters · expected impact · effort · priority · dependencies.

## 23. 30/60/90-Day Implementation Roadmap
Specific, week-organized where practical. Every item traces back to a finding elsewhere in the report — no vague "improve content" entries.

## 24. Longer-Term SEO Roadmap
Page expansion · content growth/updating/consolidation/pruning · backlinks/digital PR · location expansion · conversion optimization · monitoring · seasonal planning · measurement refinement.

## 25. Visual Evidence
Screenshots/annotations that support conclusions (rankings, Local Pack, SERPs, Lighthouse/performance findings, technical issues, competitor examples, architecture) — referenced inline where they materially help, not decorative.

## 26. Social / Platform Search Visibility
Brief, secondary. Skip in depth unless materially relevant.

## 27. Measurement Plan
What to track (GSC/GA4/leads/calls/bookings/transactions/Local Pack/GBP/reviews) and checkpoints at 30/60/90/180/365 days. Ranking alone is not the business outcome.

## 28. Scope & Next Steps
What this report includes and explicitly does not include. DIY / Implementation / Ongoing SEO paths — offered naturally, not sales-heavy.

## Confidence & Limitations
Plain-English summary of evidence strength and what's still Pending, per [report-contract.md](../report-contract.md)'s Mandatory Evidence Labeling.
