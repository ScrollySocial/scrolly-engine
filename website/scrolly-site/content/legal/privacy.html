Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Scrolly collects, why it is needed, and the choices available to you.

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Scrolly is a business operated by a sole proprietor in Texas, United States. This policy applies when you visit this website, submit a form, buy a product, join a Club or Challenge, order an SEO audit, comment on one of Scrolly's Instagram, YouTube or TikTok posts, or send Scrolly a message on Instagram.

Information we collect

  • Checkout information: details needed to process and support a purchase. Payment-card information is handled by the payment processor and is not stored directly by Scrolly.
  • Club and Challenge information: the plan details, weekly check-ins, and revenue numbers you enter in your account, sales records you submit to verify a Challenge goal, and the shirt size and shipping address needed to send an earned shirt.
  • Contact and intake information: your name, email address, website, business details, goals, and other information you choose to provide.
  • Optional read-only access: information available through services you voluntarily connect for an audit.
  • Social media comments and messages: public comments left on Scrolly's own Instagram, YouTube and TikTok posts, and direct messages sent to Scrolly on Instagram, as explained in the next section.
  • Basic website data: standard technical and analytics information about visits and site usage.

Comments and messages on social media

Scrolly uses an automation to read and answer public comments on its own Instagram, YouTube and TikTok posts, and direct messages sent to Scrolly's Instagram account (@scrollysocial).

  • What is collected: for comments, the comment text, your username or channel name, the comment and post IDs, a link to the comment, and the date it was posted. For Instagram direct messages and Story replies, the message text, your username, Instagram IDs, the time it was sent, and Scrolly's replies.
  • Why: to sort comments and messages (for example questions, requests and spam), to draft replies that Scrolly's owner reads and approves before anything is sent, and to send a free resource on Instagram when you comment, message or reply to a Story with the keyword a post asks for. That resource is sent automatically, once per request. Every other reply is approved by Scrolly's owner first.
  • Where it is stored: on Scrolly's own server at DigitalOcean, which runs the automation with n8n. It keeps technical run logs for up to 14 days and the IDs of comments and messages already handled, so nothing is answered twice. Until the move to that server is finished, comments worth answering are also saved in a private Notion database. TikTok comments are collected through Apify, and reply drafts are written with Claude by Anthropic.
  • How long it is kept: YouTube comment information is deleted within 30 days of being collected. Instagram comments and messages and TikTok comments are kept only as long as they are needed for replying and following up, and you can ask for them to be deleted at any time.
  • Who can see it: Scrolly's owner, and the service providers above while they process it. This information is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used to build profiles.

YouTube API Services

Scrolly uses YouTube API Services to read comments on its YouTube channel and post approved replies. YouTube's handling of your information is covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Only Scrolly's own YouTube channel is connected to the automation. If you have given Scrolly access to a Google account, you can remove that access at any time on the Google security settings page. Questions or complaints about how Scrolly uses YouTube information can be sent through the form below.

How information is used

Information is used to deliver purchases, run your Club and Challenge tools, verify Challenge goals, ship earned shirts, prepare requested audits, respond to messages, maintain the website, prevent misuse, and improve Scrolly products and methods. Personal information is not sold.

Service providers

Scrolly may use service providers for checkout, forms, email delivery, website hosting, analytics, SEO research, and answering social media comments and messages. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention

General inquiries may be retained for up to 24 months after resolution. Audit intake information and completed reports may be retained for up to 24 months after delivery so order questions can be addressed. Transaction records may be kept longer when required for accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, or legal purposes. Information may be deleted sooner when it is no longer reasonably needed.

Your choices and deleting your data

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information through the form below. Some records may need to be retained when required by law or for legitimate transaction and dispute purposes.

To delete your social media comments and messages: send a message through the form below, or email hello@scrollyseo.com, with the subject "Delete my social media data". Include your username or channel name and the platform (Instagram, YouTube or TikTok). Your information is deleted from Scrolly's records within 7 days, and you will get an email when it is done. This removes Scrolly's copy only; to remove a comment itself, delete it on Instagram, YouTube or TikTok.
