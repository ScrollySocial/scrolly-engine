# Template 02 Pack an order with me: sounds per beat

No sound files ship with the template: we only bundle what we can license for resale, and none of these are cleared
for that yet. The buyer adds them in CapCut in a minute, from a library their account is allowed to use.

## Where buyers can get them safely (business accounts)

- **Instagram, after export:** business accounts get a limited, mostly royalty-free library (the Meta Sound
  Collection). Search the same words there, or add a music bed from it.
- **CapCut:** Audio > Sound effects has all of the above. A business account should only use sounds CapCut marks as
  cleared for commercial use; CapCut's own terms decide this, so check the sound before posting an ad.
- **Free downloads with commercial rights:** Pixabay sound effects (Pixabay Content License: free for commercial use,
  but the files can't be resold or bundled as-is), Freesound files marked CC0 (public domain).

## If we want to ship sounds in the pack later

Only CC0 files (or sounds we record ourselves: printing a real label, typing, pouring real peanuts, taping a real box)
can go in a paid pack. Recording our own is the cleanest route and matches the real-props look.
